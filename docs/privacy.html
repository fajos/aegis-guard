1. Introduction

Welcome to AegisGuard: Ad & DNS Blocker ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Fajostech. AegisGuard is a privacy-first, on-device ad blocking and encrypted DNS management application for iOS and Android.

We believe that what you do on your personal device is your business alone. We built AegisGuard with a strict zero-knowledge architecture: we have zero access to your web traffic, zero remote tracking logs, and zero commercial data monetization.

2. Information We DO NOT Collect

Unlike traditional VPN service providers or cloud web proxies, AegisGuard does not collect or log:

  • Browsing History: We never inspect, record, or store the websites, video streams, or services you access.
  • DNS Query Logs on Remote Servers: Your DNS requests are not forwarded to or stored on our servers.
  • IP Address & Geolocation: We do not log your public, Wi-Fi, or cellular IP address.
  • Device Advertising Identifiers: We do not read or share Apple's IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) or the Android Advertising ID.
  • Personal Information: We do not require account creation, registration, or your real name and email address to use the core application.

3. How AegisGuard Works (On-Device Architecture)

AegisGuard leverages native operating system security frameworks to protect your connection without proxying data through third-party servers:

  • On iOS (Apple NetworkExtension Framework): AegisGuard configures a local DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) profile via Apple's official NEDNSSettingsManager API. DNS queries are transmitted encrypted directly to selected privacy-preserving upstream resolvers. No intermediate server can read or modify your queries.
  • On Android (Local Loopback VpnService): AegisGuard runs a virtual loopback interface (10.0.0.2) inside your phone. Packet parsing, sinkholing, and ad-blocking evaluation happen entirely on your device processor.

4. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Certain operational data is stored exclusively in your device's isolated local app storage (AsyncStorage):

  • Local Query Logs: Recent DNS queries (domain, timestamp, and blocked/allowed status) are stored in device memory for your personal review. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared instantly via the "Clear Logs" button.
  • Custom User Rules & App Exclusions: Custom filter syntax, app split-tunneling bypasses, and DNS configurations are saved locally.
  • License Entitlement: Your installation timestamp and one-off Lifetime purchase entitlement are maintained locally.

5. In-App Purchases & Billing Information

We never collect, process, or store your credit card details, bank information, or billing address.

6. Upstream DNS Resolvers & Third-Party Non-Affiliation Disclaimer

AegisGuard functions as an on-device network security and privacy configuration tool. When active, encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) requests are resolved by independent, publicly accessible Anycast DNS networks:

  • Default Protection (Aegis Shield Profile): Powered by publicly accessible Anycast privacy resolvers (including AdGuard Public DNS infrastructure) to provide automated ad-blocking, tracker prevention, and family protection. These resolvers operate under strict zero-knowledge principles and do not log user IP addresses or browsing histories.
  • Basic Mode: Routes through Cloudflare Public DNS (1.1.1.1), an independent high-speed encrypted resolver with public no-logs commitments.
  • Custom Resolvers: Users retain full freedom to configure any custom DoH, DoT, or DNSCrypt server of their choice.
