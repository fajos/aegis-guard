Aegis Guard does NOT collect, store, profile, monitor, or share your DNS queries, browsing history, IP address, or personal data. All network filtering occurs 100% on your device hardware or via encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH).

1. Introduction

Welcome to Aegis Guard ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Fajostechnologies Ltd. Aegis Guard is a privacy-first, on-device ad blocking and encrypted DNS management application for iOS and Android.

We believe that what you do on your personal device is your business alone. We built Aegis Guard with a strict zero-knowledge architecture: we have zero access to your web traffic, zero remote tracking logs, and zero commercial data monetization.

2. Information We DO NOT Collect

Unlike traditional VPN service providers or cloud web proxies, Aegis Guard does not collect or log:

  • Browsing History: We never inspect, record, or store the websites, video streams, or services you access.
  • DNS Query Logs on Remote Servers: Your DNS requests are not forwarded to or stored on our servers.
  • IP Address & Geolocation: We do not log your public, Wi-Fi, or cellular IP address.
  • Device Advertising Identifiers: We do not read or share Apple's IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) or the Android Advertising ID.
  • Personal Information: We do not require account creation, registration, or your real name and email address to use the core application.

3. How Aegis Guard Works (On-Device Architecture)

Aegis Guard leverages native operating system security frameworks to protect your connection without proxying data through third-party servers:

  • On iOS (Apple NetworkExtension Framework): Aegis Guard configures a local DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) profile via Apple's official NEDNSSettingsManager API. DNS queries are transmitted encrypted directly to selected privacy-preserving upstream resolvers (e.g. AdGuard DNS or Cloudflare DoH). No intermediate server can read or modify your queries.
  • On Android (Local Loopback VpnService): Aegis Guard runs a virtual loopback interface (10.0.0.2) inside your phone. Packet parsing, sinkholing, and ad-blocking evaluation happen entirely on your device processor.

4. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Certain operational data is stored exclusively in your device's isolated local app storage (AsyncStorage):

  • Local Query Logs: Recent DNS queries (domain, timestamp, and blocked/allowed status) are stored in device memory for your personal review. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared instantly via the "Clear Logs" button.
  • Custom User Rules & App Exclusions: Custom filter syntax, app split-tunneling bypasses, and DNS configurations are saved locally.
  • License Entitlement: Your installation timestamp and one-off Lifetime purchase entitlement are maintained locally.

5. In-App Purchases & Billing Information

Aegis Guard offers an optional Lifetime Pro license as a one-time in-app purchase. All financial transactions and payment processing are handled exclusively by Apple App Store (StoreKit) or Google Play Billing. We never collect, process, or store your credit card details, bank information, or billing address.

6. Upstream DNS Resolvers

When Aegis Guard is active, encrypted DNS lookups are sent to the upstream resolver you choose in Settings:

  • AdGuard DNS (Default): Provides ad-blocking, tracker prevention, and family protection without recording user IP addresses or search histories.
  • Cloudflare DNS (Basic Mode): High-speed encrypted resolver (1.1.1.1) audited by independent third-party privacy firms.
  • Custom Resolvers: Users may configure any custom DoH, DoT, or DNSCrypt endpoint.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Aegis Guard does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. An optional Family Shield Mode is provided to help parents block adult content, malware, and explicit domains across all apps on the device.
